What We Do

Full-Spectrum Logistics for the Energy Sector

From petroleum haulage to marine chandelling, we handle the operational backbone so you can focus on your core business.

Petroleum Product Haulage

NMDPRA and NUPRC-licensed haulage of petroleum products (white and black) across Nigeria. Fully compliant with the Petroleum Industry Act 2021.

Ship Chandelling

Timely supply of food, provisions, beverages, and cleaning materials to ocean-going vessels and offshore platforms, ensuring crew welfare and operational continuity.

Procurement & Supply

Sourcing and delivery of OCTG, casing & tubing, marine equipment, communication equipment, and industrial materials to oil & gas operators.

Leasing & Asset Management

Provision of vehicles, marine vessels, houseboats, boats, and field equipment for project and operational support. Ready-to-deploy, cost-effective solutions.

Airport Protocol & Travel Logistics

Professional coordination of airport reception, immigration assistance, and personnel travel logistics for oil & gas industry executives and field staff.

Consultancy & Commodity Supply

Advisory services across marine logistics, procurement planning, and operational optimization, plus distribution of essential commodities to public and private sector clients.
